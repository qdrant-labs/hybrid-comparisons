Only 100 queries sampled here (vs. 500 for msmarco/dbpedia) to match scifact's much smaller 15.4K-passage corpus. At this sample size, mrr@20/ndcg@20 differences of a few points between quantization configs are dominated by which handful of queries land at a slightly different rank, not by a genuine quantization effect — recall@20 is the more stable metric to read config-to-config here. The per-rescorer comparisons (colbert vs cross-encoder vs rrf, hybrid vs sparse-only) are averaged across all 8 configs and read the same way as the other two reports.
