Does the dense/colbert datatype affect colbert rescoring itself?

Colbert only, all 8 configs, k=20 prefetch_limit=100. Recall barely moves (already shown above) — mrr and ndcg tell a different story: turbo4 (both plain and with tq1 quantization on top) sits about 1–1.5 pt below float32/float16/int8 in both prefetch modes, roughly 1–2% relative. The right document still lands in the top-k almost as often; turbo4's lower precision costs it a bit of fine-grained ranking order, not retrieval.

Worth being precise about what turbo4 is here: unlike the binary/scalar/product configs, which keep a full-precision copy of the vectors alongside a quantized one purely for candidate search, turbo4 is native storage — every colbert token vector lives at 4 bits per dimension, full stop, no float32 copy sitting next to it. That's an 8× reduction in per-vector storage for a ~1–2% hit on ranking quality and no measurable hit on recall.
